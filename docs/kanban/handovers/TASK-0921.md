### What was built:
1. **Core Mini-Games Session Framework (`@ririko/services/games`)**:
   - `packages/services/src/games/types.ts`: typed models for `GameSession`, `GameType` (`TICTACTOE`, `RPS`, `HIGHLOW`, `COINFLIP`, `DICE`), `GameState` (`PENDING_OPPONENT`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `COMPLETED`, `TIED`, `TIMEOUT`, `CANCELLED`), `Player`, `WagerConfig`, and `CreateSessionOptions`.
   - `packages/services/src/games/session-manager.ts`: thread-safe in-memory session manager with:
     - 1 active game per user per channel concurrency guard.
     - Turn and session expiration timer tracking (`checkTimeouts`).
     - Listener hooks for timeout event routing (`onTimeout`).
     - Clean lifecycle transitions and user channel unbinding on completion/forfeiture.
2. **Unbeatable Minimax Tic-Tac-Toe Engine (`@ririko/services/games/tictactoe`)**:
   - `minimax.ts`:
     - Mathematical recursive Minimax algorithm with depth-discounted scoring (`10 - depth` for AI win, `depth - 10` for human win, `0` for tie) and alpha-beta pruning.
     - Unbeatable proof: When AI plays against itself, result is always a tie. When AI plays against any suboptimal human moves, AI either wins or ties, but never loses.
     - Optimal opening move fast-path: takes center cell 4 on empty board.
     - `checkWinner`: checks all 8 winning lines (3 rows, 3 columns, 2 diagonals) and tie condition.
   - `engine.ts`:
     - `TicTacToeEngine`: coordinates PvE (vs Minimax AI) and PvP (human vs human) games.
     - Validates cell bounds, cell occupancy, turn sequence.
     - Automatically invokes Minimax move and updates board state if opponent is AI.
     - Supports timeout / forfeiture handling.
3. **Rock-Paper-Scissors Secret Selection Engine (`@ririko/services/games/rps`)**:
   - `types.ts` & `engine.ts`:
     - Secret ephemeral choice submission: individual moves are stored secretly and masked until both players have submitted.
     - `evaluateOutcome`: standard circular win rules (Rock > Scissors, Scissors > Paper, Paper > Rock, identical = Tie).
     - PvE support: AI secret choice pre-generated secretly and revealed upon human submission.
     - Timeout forfeiture: if one player submits and the other times out, submitter wins by forfeiture.
4. **Wager Escrow & Settlement Engine (`@ririko/services/games/escrow`)**:
   - `escrow.ts`:
     - `GameEscrowService`: integrates with `EconomyRepository`.
     - Validates balances and frozen status of human players.
     - Atomically holds wagers at game start (`GAME_ESCROW`).
     - Settles wagers upon game finish: awards 2x total pot to human winner (`GAME_WIN`); refunds escrowed wagers on tie (`GAME_ESCROW_REFUND`); refunds on cancellation.

## 3. Gotchas & Key Decisions
- **`exactOptionalPropertyTypes` in TypeScript**:
  - In `CreateSessionOptions`, optional fields like `wagerAmount`, `timeoutMs`, `id`, and `currentTurnPlayerId` must have `| undefined` explicitly specified to support optional passed variables.
- **Minimax Alpha-Beta Pruning**:
  - Because Tic-Tac-Toe branching factor is at most 9!, alpha-beta pruning allows finding the optimal move in milliseconds.
- **Deterministic AI Choice in RPS**:
  - Injected `rngFn` parameter allows 100% deterministic testing without random flakes.
