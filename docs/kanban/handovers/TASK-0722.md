## 2. Key Deliverables & Implemented Architecture

### 2.1. Anti-Raid Mass Join Monitor & Server Verification Gate (`AntiRaidService`)
Located in `packages/services/src/moderation/anti-raid.service.ts`:
- **Sliding-Window Join Velocity Monitor**:
  - Monitors member joins per guild with configurable thresholds (`joinThreshold` joins within `windowSeconds` seconds; default: 10 joins in 10s).
  - Automatically isolates and prunes expired window entries to maintain $O(1)$ memory usage.
- **Account Creation Age Heuristics**:
  - Inspects account age upon arrival: `(now - accountCreatedTimestamp) / (1000 * 3600)`.
  - Flags accounts created $< 24$ hours ago as suspicious fresh accounts.
  - Detects coordinated bot-net flood attacks when $\ge 5$ fresh accounts join within the window, triggering immediate defensive countermeasures even if total joins are under the bulk velocity threshold.
- **Raid Mitigation State Machine**:
  - Transitions through `NORMAL` $\to$ `RAID_DETECTED` $\to$ `LOCKDOWN`.
  - Supports configurable defensive actions (`LOCKDOWN`, `VERIFICATION_GATE`, `ALERT_ONLY`).
  - Implements automatic recovery (`cooldownMinutes`, default: 10 minutes) to transition guilds safely back to `NORMAL` state once the raid subsides.
  - Supports manual administrative override via `resolveRaid(guildId)`.
- **Audit Logging, Embed Alerts & EventBus Integration**:
  - Automatically records audit cases in `moderation_cases` (`type: 'LOCKDOWN'`, `moderatorUserId: 'ANTI_RAID'`).
  - Dispatches `moderation:raidDetected` to the `EventBus`.
  - Generates rich staff alert embeds with metric counts, sample flagged accounts with age in hours, status countdown, and high-visibility alert coloring.
