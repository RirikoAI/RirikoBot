## 1. Summary of Work Accomplished

### 1.1. Voice Anti-AFK Accumulator State Machine ([`packages/services/src/economy/voice-accumulator.ts`](file:///z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/economy/voice-accumulator.ts))
Implemented the full voice participation engine meeting Section 35 of `BLUEPRINT.md` and Section 3.2 of `docs/economy.md`:
- **Participant Quorum Engine**:
  - Requires a minimum of **2 active humans** (`minQuorum`, configurable, default: 2) in a voice channel.
  - Channels with $< 2$ active humans immediately fail quorum, awarding 0 XP.
- **Strict Disqualification Filters**:
  - Members are disqualified from quorum and rewards if:
    - `isBot === true`
    - `isSelfMuted === true`
    - `isSelfDeafened === true`
    - `isServerMuted === true`
    - `isServerDeafened === true`
- **AFK Channel Hard-Exclusions**:
  - Channels configured via `setAfkChannel(channelId)` or `afkChannelIds` are completely excluded from accrual regardless of participant count.
- **Discrete Accrual & Remainder Preservation**:
  - Accrues active participation time in discrete 60-second intervals (`intervalSeconds`).
  - Upon reaching 60s, produces an `EconomyEvent` with `type: EconomyEventType.VOICE_MINUTE`, deducting 60s while preserving any excess remainder seconds.
  - Multi-minute jumps automatically compute `minutesEarned = Math.floor(accrued / intervalSeconds)`.
- **Channel Migration & Leave Handling**:
  - `onVoiceStateUpdate` handles user joins, channel transitions, mute/deafen toggles, and channel cleanups.
  - `handleUserLeave(userId)` cleanly removes the participant and purges session buffers.
- **Direct Economy Service Integration**:
  - Automatically dispatches `VOICE_MINUTE` events through `EconomyService.handleEvent(event)` when an instance is provided, committing double-entry ledger transactions and updating balances.
