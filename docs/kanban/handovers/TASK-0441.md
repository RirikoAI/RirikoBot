   - **Dimensions & Aesthetic**: $1200 \times 400$ px canvas resolution with rounded clipping (20px radius) and subtle glass gradient border.

     - `WALLET CREDITS` (`🪙 <formatted>`)
     - `BANK BALANCE` (`🏦 <formatted>`)
     - `TOTAL EXP` (`⭐ <formatted>`)
   - **XP Progress Bar**: Rounded pill track with glossy gradient fill (`#00f2fe` -> `#4facfe` -> `#8b5cf6`), exact percentage display, and XP required for next level.
   - **Waifu TCG Collectible Card Showcase**: Dedicated right-hand card frame ($205 \times 336$ px) supporting equipped card portrait, 8-tier rarity color border & tag, serial number tag, and card title banner; with an elegant dashed placeholder and `/tcg equip` command hint when no card is equipped.
