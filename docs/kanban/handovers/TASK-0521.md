## 1. Summary of Completed Work
Implemented the comprehensive 17-command dual-dispatch music commands suite in `apps/bot` with `MusicPlayerService` integration and full parity with legacy Ririko bot:

1. **Music Player Service (`packages/music/src/player/music-player.service.ts`)**:
   - Manages audio playback, `GuildQueue` instances, and `VoiceLifecycleManager` connections.
   - Subscribes `AudioPlayer` to `@discordjs/voice` connections and plays resolved audio streams using `createAudioResource`.
   - Handles player lifecycle (`play`, `pause`, `resume`, `skip`, `back`, `stop`, `seek`, `setVolume`, `setLoop`, `shuffle`, `setFilter`, `clearFilters`).
   - Dispatches audio stream errors to `queue.emit('error')` with automatic skip recovery.
2. **Dual-Dispatch Music Command Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/music/commands.ts`)**:
   - Registered 17 commands under `CommandCategory.MUSIC` with guild-only enforcement and dual slash/prefix support:
     1. `/play <query>`: Joins voice channel, resolves URL or search keywords via `ExtractorPipeline`, queues track/playlist, and records history into `music_history`.
     2. `/pause`: Pauses currently playing track with state validation.
     3. `/resume`: Resumes paused track.
     4. `/skip [to]`: Skips current track or jumps directly to a specific 1-indexed queue position.
     5. `/back`: Restores and plays the previous track from queue history.
     6. `/stop`: Stops playback, clears queue, resets state, and leaves the voice channel.
     7. `/queue [page]`: Rich embed displaying current track with progress bar (`▬🔘▬`) and paginated upcoming tracks (10 per page) with total duration.
     8. `/nowplaying`: Detailed embed of the current playing track with requester, source, volume, loop mode, active filters, and interactive-ready details.
     9. `/volume <percent>`: Adjusts audio gain with safety clamping ($0 \le v \le 150$) and persists `defaultVolume` into `music_guild_settings`.
     10. `/loop [mode]`: Cycles through or directly sets `OFF`, `TRACK`, or `QUEUE` loop modes.
     11. `/shuffle`: Fisher-Yates shuffle of all upcoming tracks in the queue.
     12. `/seek <timestamp>`: Parses `mm:ss` or `hh:mm:ss` format and seeks to desired offset.
     13. `/filter <preset>`: Toggles audio filters (`bassboost`, `nightcore`, `vaporwave`, `8d`, `karaoke`, `tremolo`, `vibrato`, `surround`) or resets with `normal`.
     14. `/lyrics [query]`: Searches Genius / LRCLIB provider for track lyrics with automatic message splitting (2000 chars).
     15. `/join`: Summons bot to the caller's voice channel.
     16. `/leave`: Disconnects bot from voice channel and resets queue.
     17. `/playlist <subcommand>`: Full custom playlist management (`create`, `list`, `add`, `play`, `delete`) backed by `MusicRepository`.
3. **Integration & Routing**:
   - `createBotServices` in `apps/bot/src/services.ts` wires `MusicRepository` and `MusicPlayerService`.
   - `main.ts` registers all 17 commands into the central command router.

## 3. Key Design Decisions & Gotchas
- **Voice Adapter Creator in Mocks**: `@discordjs/voice` requires `adapterCreator` to be a valid callable function `() => ({ sendPayload, destroy })`.
- **Direct Voice Re-exports**: `VoiceConnection` and `DiscordGatewayAdapterCreator` are re-exported directly from `@ririko/music` so consumer packages (`@ririko/bot`) don't need a direct peer dependency on `@discordjs/voice`.
