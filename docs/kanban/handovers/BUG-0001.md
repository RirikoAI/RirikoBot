## 1. Summary of Work Accomplished

### Root Cause
All source adapters in `packages/music/src/extractors` previously contained placeholder stubs returning an empty `Readable` stream that pushed `null` on the first read. This immediately triggered `AudioPlayerStatus.Idle`, causing the queue to conclude the song had finished in 0ms and stop without emitting any sound. Additionally, platform URLs were resolved into hardcoded fake dummy titles.

## 3. Decisions & Gotchas

1. **YouTube SABR & BotGuard Defense**:
   Official music videos on YouTube use Server-Side Adaptive Bitrate (SABR / UMP) and BotGuard attestation, causing unauthenticated downloads to throw `No valid URL to decipher` or 403 Forbidden. The multi-tier cascade addresses this by:
   - Permitting optional user cookies or PO-tokens in `.env`.
   - Resolving Topic/audio tracks internally on YouTube.
   - Seamlessly falling back to SoundCloud when direct YouTube formats are locked.
2. **Spotify DRM Audio**:
   Spotify does not provide raw public audio streams; metadata extraction via `spotify-url-info` coupled with SoundCloud/YouTube audio mapping provides seamless playback of Spotify playlists and albums.
