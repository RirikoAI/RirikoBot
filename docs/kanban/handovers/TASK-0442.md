## 2. Implemented Features & Changes
1. **Dependency Wiring & Services Container (`apps/bot/src/services.ts`)**:
   - Added `@ririko/services: "workspace:*"` to `apps/bot/package.json`.
   - Implemented `createBotServices(client: DatabaseClient)` instantiating and wiring all repositories and services:
     - `userRepo`, `economyRepo`, `xpRepo`, `guildSettingsRepo`, `leaderboardRepo`, `itemRepo`, `inventoryRepo`, `playerEnergyRepo`.
     - `economyService`, `bankingService`, `dailyService`, `levelingService`, `leaderboardService`, `inventoryService`, `profileBackgroundManager`, `profileCardRenderer`, `antiSpamEvaluator`, `voiceSessionAccumulator`.
2. **Dual-Dispatch Discord Commands Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/economy/`)**:
   - Implemented 11 economy commands with complete parity across Slash interactions and legacy text prefixes:
     - `/balance`: Displays current wallet, bank, and net worth balances (self or @target).
     - `/daily`: Claims daily currency reward with streak multipliers and informs about next claim window.
     - `/deposit`: Safely deposits credits into the bank (specific amount or `all`).
     - `/withdraw`: Withdraws credits from bank into wallet (specific amount or `all`).
     - `/pay`: P2P credit transfer with self-transfer and negative amount guards.
     - `/leaderboard`: Paginated server-level or global XP leaderboards.
     - `/profile`: Generates and attaches the high-resolution 1200x400 Profile Card 2.0 PNG rendering.
     - `/shop`: Interactive town shop catalog with item details and purchase dispatch.
     - `/inventory`: Formatted player inventory bag listing owned quantities and item rarities.
     - `/use`: Consumes items from inventory with real-time effect application (energy potions, XP potions, credits, profile vouchers).
     - `/karma`: Inspects karma points and awards karma to fellow community members.
3. **Gateway Event Listeners (`apps/bot/src/listeners/`)**:
   - **`messageCreate` (`apps/bot/src/listeners/message.listener.ts`)**:
     - Connects `AntiSpamEvaluator` to reject bot messages, DM channels, spam, and rapid repetitions.
     - Awards random 15-25 XP and dispatches `CHAT_MESSAGE` economy rewards via `EconomyService.handleEvent`.
     - Automatically notifies users upon leveling up if guild and user settings allow.
   - **`voiceStateUpdate` (`apps/bot/src/listeners/voice.listener.ts`)**:
     - Connects `VoiceSessionAccumulator` tracking channel joins, leaves, and switches.
     - Accumulates voice XP while enforcing anti-AFK quorum and mute/deafen disqualifications.
4. **Main Bot Entrypoints (`apps/bot/src/main.ts`, `apps/bot/src/index.ts`)**:
   - Exported and integrated `createBotServices`, `createEconomyCommands`, `registerMessageListener`, `registerVoiceListener` into bot startup.
5. **Unit & Integration Test Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/economy/commands.test.ts`)**:
   - 17 comprehensive test cases verifying all 11 commands, edge cases (insufficient funds, negative amounts, self-transfers, pagination), and gateway listener registrations with in-memory SQLite database.
