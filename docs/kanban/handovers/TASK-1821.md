## GROOMING · 2026-10-09T14:32:04Z · claude-opus-5-5 (coordinator)

**Approach**
1. `apps/web/src/lib/dashboard-nav.ts`: export `GUILD_NAV_GROUPS` (ordered `{ id, label }` list: server, moderation, members, engagement, tcg, features) and add `group` to every `GUILD_NAV_ITEMS` entry. Reorder the entries so each group's links are contiguous, in the order the acceptance lists. Keep `overview` first, and keep `as const`.
2. `apps/web/src/components/guild-nav.tsx`: keep the current-slug logic. Render two views:
   - From `md` up: one `<div>` per group with a small heading (for example `text-xs font-semibold uppercase tracking-wide text-zinc-500`, not a link) and the group's `<ul>` of links. Tighten link padding (`py-1.5`) and the gaps.
   - Below `md`: a `<label>` ("Server settings page" or similar, visually hidden is fine) and a native `<select>` with one `<optgroup label=…>` per group and one `<option value={href}>` per item, `value` set to the current page's href. `onChange` calls `useRouter().push(event.target.value)`.
   - Hide each view at the other breakpoint (`hidden md:block` and `md:hidden`). Keep `<nav aria-label="Server settings">` as the wrapper.
3. `apps/web/src/app/dashboard/[guildId]/layout.tsx`: make the aside sticky on desktop with its own scroll, for example `md:sticky md:top-4 md:max-h-[calc(100vh-2rem)] md:self-start md:overflow-y-auto`. `md:self-start` is needed, or the grid stretches the aside and sticky does nothing.
4. Tests:
   - `apps/web/src/lib/dashboard-nav.test.ts` (node): every item has a group from `GUILD_NAV_GROUPS`, every group has at least one item, slugs are unique, `GUILD_NAV_ITEMS[0].slug === 'overview'`.
   - `apps/web/src/components/guild-nav.test.tsx` (`// @vitest-environment jsdom`): mock `next/navigation` with `usePathname` and `useRouter` (pattern: `apps/web/src/components/account-interactions.test.tsx:15`). Check the headings, `aria-current="page"` on `/dashboard/<id>/tcg/achievements` (and not on `tcg`), the select's optgroups and options, the selected value, and that changing it calls `router.push` with the href.

**Pitfalls**
- Do not rename labels or slugs: the notifier and existing links depend on them. "TCG Achievements" keeps its label even under the Waifu TCG heading.
- `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` and `as const`: type the group field from `GUILD_NAV_GROUPS` ids so a typo fails typecheck.
- The sticky aside needs `md:self-start`; without it the aside is as tall as the grid row and never sticks.
- A test that renders both views will see each link twice (list and option). Query the list by role `link` and the picker by role `combobox`.
- Run `pnpm format:check` before handing back; the lint-staged hook will not run for the worker.

**Out of scope**
- Collapsible groups, a filter box, icons, or merging the Welcome and Farewell pages.
- The owner console and account navs (`owner-nav.tsx`, `account-nav.tsx`).
- Any page content changes.
