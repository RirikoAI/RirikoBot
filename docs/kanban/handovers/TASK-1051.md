# TASK-1051 Handover: Atomic P2P Trading, State Locking & Community Marketplace Engine with Tax & Expiration

## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Dual-Dialect Repositories (`packages/database`)**:
  - `CardTradeRepository` (`packages/database/src/repositories/card-trade.repository.ts`): Full CRUD for `card_trades` table across SQLite and PostgreSQL, with normalization of credit values (`offeredCredits`, `requestedCredits`), pending user trade queries, and active trade mutual lookups.
  - `MarketListingRepository` (`packages/database/src/repositories/market-listing.repository.ts`): Full CRUD for `market_listings` table, active listings queries with pagination, seller listings lookup, and 7-day expiration detection (`findExpiredListings`).
  - Added `updateUserCardOwner` in `WaifuCardRepository` to facilitate atomic card transfers between users.
- **P2P Trading Engine (`TradeService` in `packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/trading/trade-service.ts`)**:
  - Propose trades between two users with offered/requested cards and credit amounts.
  - **State Locking**: Offered and requested cards are locked to `state = 'IN_TRADE'` upon proposal creation.
  - **Dual Confirmation & Atomic Transfer**: In a single ACID database transaction, `acceptTrade` transfers credits via `EconomyRepository.transferBalance`, updates card owners via `updateUserCardOwner`, and unlocks cards back to `IDLE`.
  - **Rejection & Cancellation**: Reverts locked cards back to `IDLE` state.
- **Community Player Marketplace (`MarketService` in `packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/market/market-service.ts`)**:
  - Lists cards for custom credit prices with `state = 'IN_MARKET'` state locking.
  - **5% Market Tax Sink**: Automatically calculates and retains 5% listing fee on sale (`taxPaid = Math.floor(price * 0.05)`).
  - **7-Day Expiration**: `processExpiredListings` finds expired listings, marks them `EXPIRED`, and reverts cards to `IDLE`.
  - **Atomic Purchase**: Buyer pays full price, seller receives net amount (`price - tax`), and card ownership transfers to buyer in `IDLE` state.
- **Dual-Dispatch Bot Commands (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg`)**:
  - `/trade` suite (`trade.command.ts`): `request`, `accept`, `reject`, `cancel`, `view`, `list` with full slash and prefix parity (`!trade ...`).
  - `/market` suite (`market.command.ts`): `list`, `browse`, `buy`, `cancel`, `my-listings` with full slash and prefix parity (`!market ...`).
  - Wired into `apps/bot/src/services.ts` and registered in `apps/bot/src/main.ts`.
