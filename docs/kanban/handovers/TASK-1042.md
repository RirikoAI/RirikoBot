## 1. Summary of Changes
- **Tutorial Onboarding Engine (`TutorialService`)**:
  - Implemented 4 prologue tutorial floors (T1: Elemental Resonance, T2: Mana & Active Skills, T3: Consumables & Survival, T4: Boss Break Shields) in `packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/dungeon/tutorial-service.ts`.
  - Zero Energy cost (introductory free access).
  - First-clear completion grants:
    - Starter Waifu Card: *Flame Novice Aria [FIRE]*
    - Common Weapon: *Novice Blade (+20 ATK)*
    - Consumables: *3x Minor HP Potions (+250 HP)*
    - Achievement Unlock: `TUTORIAL_COMPLETE`

    - Floors 26–40: 20 Energy
    - Floors 41–50+: 25 Energy
- **Loot Drops Engine (`DungeonLootService`)**:
  - Implemented milestone first-clear rewards in `packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/dungeon/dungeon-loot.service.ts`:
    - Floor 10: 2,500 Credits, 100 EXP, 50 Crafting Dust, Rare Weapon *Iron Greatsword*
    - Floor 20: 5,000 Credits, 250 EXP, 150 Crafting Dust, 1x Summon Ticket, Rare Relic *Chrono Fragment*
    - Floor 30: 10,000 Credits, 500 EXP, 300 Crafting Dust, 2x Summon Tickets, SR Weapon *Glacial Edge*
    - Floor 40: 20,000 Credits, 1,000 EXP, 600 Crafting Dust, 3x Summon Tickets, UR Accessory *Prismatic Ring*
    - Floor 50: 50,000 Credits, 2,500 EXP, 1,500 Crafting Dust, 5x Summon Tickets, Mythic Title *Tower Vanquisher*, UR Armor *Glacial Aegis*
  - Repeat floor loot with RNG scaling (Credits, EXP, Crafting Dust, Minor/Major Potions).
  - Wired into `DungeonRunner` to dispatch automatically on floor victory.
- **Dual-Dispatch `/dungeon` Commands Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/dungeon.command.ts`)**:
  - `/dungeon status`: Overview of highest floor cleared, current active season, environmental affixes, and available energy.
  - `/dungeon climb [floor_number]`: Initiates floor combat with equipped cards, checks energy, triggers ward mechanics and soft enrage, displays turn logs, and awards loot on victory.
  - `/dungeon floor <floor_number>`: Inspects floor enemy stats (HP, ATK, DEF, SPD), boss multipliers, barrier defenses, and strategic checks.
  - `/dungeon leaderboard`: Displays top 10 season tower climbers.
  - `/dungeon tutorial`: Runs prologue onboarding and dispatches starter pack.
  - Full slash and prefix command parity (`!dungeon`, `!dungeon climb`, `!dungeon floor <num>`, `!dungeon leaderboard`, `!dungeon tutorial`).
- **Bot Wiring & Entrypoints**:
  - Wired repositories and services in `apps/bot/src/services.ts`.
  - Registered command in `apps/bot/src/main.ts` and exported in `apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/index.ts`.
