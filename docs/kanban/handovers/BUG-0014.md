## 1. Summary of Work Accomplished

**Root cause.** Gear is linked to a card through `user_inventory_items.equipped_to_card_id`, and the gear row keeps its own `user_id`. Market sales and trades only changed the card's owner, and dismantle deleted the card. Nothing touched the gear:
- The buyer's card fought with the seller's gear, because `findEquippedByCard` does not filter by owner.
- The seller's gear stayed `EQUIPPED` on a card they no longer owned. It never showed in the gear menu, and recovering it needed an item ID that no screen shows.
- Dismantled cards left gear pointing at a deleted card.

**Decision (user's choice):** only empty cards change hands. Gear is never transferred with a card.

**Changes**
- [gear-lock.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/equipment/gear-lock.ts) (new):
  - `listEquippedSlots` lists the slots of a card that hold gear owned by the card's owner.
  - `assertCardHasNoGear` throws a player-facing error. It names the card, the occupied slots, the reason (gear never moves with a card), and the fix (`/card action:unequip-all id:<id>` or **Unequip All** in `/loadout`).
- [market-service.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/market/market-service.ts):
  - `listCard` refuses cards with gear.
  - `buyListing` re-checks inside the purchase transaction, so a listing made before this fix can't sell gear.
  - The constructor takes `inventoryRepo` (5th argument, before `options`).
- [trade-service.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/trading/trade-service.ts):
  - `createProposal` refuses offered or requested cards with gear. For a requested card, the message tells the player to ask its owner to unequip it.
  - `acceptTrade` re-checks every card inside the swap transaction.
  - The constructor takes `inventoryRepo`.
- [loadout-service.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/equipment/loadout-service.ts):
  - `unequipAll(userId, cardId?)` empties one card. Without `cardId` it empties every card, including gear stranded on cards the user no longer owns.
  - `equip` refuses `IN_MARKET` and `IN_TRADE` cards, so gear can't be added after a card is listed or offered.
  - `getCardLoadout` only counts gear owned by the card's owner. Gear already stranded by older sales no longer lends stats to the new owner.
- [tcg-item.repository.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/repositories/tcg-item.repository.ts): `unequipAllForUser(userId, cardId?, tx?)` is a single bulk update (SQLite + PG).
- [dismantle-service.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/card/dismantle-service.ts): returns the card's gear to the inventory before deleting the card. It reports `gearReturned`, and the constructor now takes `inventoryRepo` as its 2nd argument.
- Bot:
  - `/card action:unequip-all [id]` (prefix `card unequip-all [id]`).
  - An **Unequip All** button in the `/loadout` gear menu (`gear:unequip_all`), disabled when the card is empty.
  - The dismantle reply mentions returned gear.
  - Services are wired in `services.ts`.
- Guide and docs:
  - `/tcg-info` gear, trade and market topics explain the empty-card rule and unequip-all.
  - `docs/commands.md` and `docs/waifu-tcg.md` §8.1/§8.2 are updated.

## 3. Roadblocks, Gotchas & Decisions Made
- Dismantle **returns** gear instead of blocking. The owner doesn't change, so there's nothing to leak, and blocking would only add friction.
- Stranded gear from sales before this fix is **not** repaired automatically. Its owner recovers it with `/card action:unequip-all` (no ID), and it no longer affects the new owner's card either way. A boot-time repair can be added if live data shows many stranded rows.
- A listing or pending trade created before this fix, where the card still has gear, now fails at buy or accept time with the gear message. The seller must cancel, unequip and relist.
- Error text starts with 🔒 and the bot prefixes ❌, so players see both.
