## 2. Technical Scope & Implementation Completed

### 2.1. Energy Lifecycle Engine (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/energy/energy-lifecycle.service.ts`)
- **Formula Enforcement ([Section 12.2](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/docs/waifu-tcg.md#L407-L417))**:
  $$\text{Capacity} = 100 + \lfloor \text{PlayerLevel} \times 1.5 \rfloor + \text{MilestoneBonuses}$$
  - Milestone bonuses applied at levels:
    - Lv.10: +5
    - Lv.25: +15
    - Lv.50: +30
    - Lv.75: +50
    - Lv.100: +75
- **Lazy Daily Rollover Reconciliation ([Section 12.1](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/docs/waifu-tcg.md#L400-L405))**:
  - Reconciles energy upon user activity without background cron spam.
  - Automatically resets daily stamina potions counter `dailyEnergyPotsUsed = 0` upon date change.
  - Preserves energy overflow if current energy exceeds normal cap (e.g. from potions or events).
- **Consumption & Replenishment**:
  - Validates sufficient balance before deducting action costs (Expeditions, Boss Raids, PvP).
  - Provides `consumeEnergy` and `replenishEnergy` methods with audit trails.

### 2.3. Dual-Dispatch Bot Commands & Combat Integration
1. **`/item` Command Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/item.command.ts`)**:
   - `inventory` (`/item action:inventory [filter]`): Displays paginated player inventory, filtering by equipment, accessories, or consumables.
   - `enhance` (`/item action:enhance id:<id>`): Upgrades gear pieces from +0 to +10, displaying new stats and scaled perks while deducting Crafting Dust and Credits.
   - `use` (`/item action:use id:<id>`): Consumes HP potions, Mana potions, and Stamina potions with daily 3/day anti-abuse validation.
2. **`/card` Gear Loadouts (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/card.command.ts`)**:
   - `loadout` (`/card action:loadout [id]`): Renders visual 6-slot loadout embed showing equipped Weapon, Armor, Relic, Ring, Amulet, Talisman, aggregate stat bonuses, and active battle perks.
   - `equip-gear` (`/card action:equip-gear id:<card_id> item_id:<item_id> slot:<slot>`): Equips gear piece into target slot, swapping out existing piece to inventory.
   - `unequip-gear` (`/card action:unequip-gear item_id:<item_id>`): Safely unequips gear piece back to summoner inventory.
3. **`/game` Shop & Combat Wiring (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/game.command.ts`)**:
   - `shop` (`/game action:shop [subaction]`): Displays Town Shop catalog with prices, perks, and daily limits.
   - `buy` (`/game action:buy item:<code_or_id> [quantity]`): Purchases items using credits and generates audited receipt.
   - **Combatant Loadout Injection**: Augmented `fetchUserCombatCards` in `game.command.ts` to fetch equipped loadouts via `LoadoutService` and apply stat bonuses and perks directly to combatants before Expedition, Boss, or PvP simulations.
