## 1. Summary of Work Accomplished
**Symptom:** a guild that puts a join-to-create hub in a category with permanent voice channels loses those channels whenever they are empty.

**Root cause:** `AutoVoiceService` decided what to delete by category, not by ownership.
- On member leave, a channel missing from the in-memory `activeChannels` map was deleted if it was empty and shared a category with any hub.
- `cleanupOrphans` (called for every guild on READY) deleted every empty voice channel in a hub's category except the hub.
- `activeChannels` lived only in memory, so after a restart the service could not tell its channels from the server's own. The category scan was the workaround, and it was the bug.

**Fix:** the service now records every channel it creates and deletes only recorded channels.
- New dual-dialect table `auto_voice_channels` (`channel_id` PK, `guild_id`, `parent_channel_id`, `created_at`, index on `guild_id`), next to `auto_voice_configs`:
  - [sqlite/utilities.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/schema/sqlite/utilities.ts)
  - [pg/utilities.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/schema/pg/utilities.ts)
  - Types `AutoVoiceChannel` / `NewAutoVoiceChannel` in [schema/types/index.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/schema/types/index.ts)
  - Regenerated [sqlite/ddl.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/schema/sqlite/ddl.ts) with `pnpm -F @ririko/database db:generate-ddl` (only the new table and index were added).
- New [autovoice-channel.repository.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/database/src/repositories/autovoice-channel.repository.ts) (`AutoVoiceChannelRepository`: `findById`, `listByGuildId`, `exists`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `count`), exported from the repositories index.
- [autovoice/service.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/packages/services/src/autovoice/service.ts):
  - The constructor takes `AutoVoiceChannelRepository` as its second argument.
  - Creation records the channel right after `guild.channels.create`. If a later step fails, the channel is deleted and the record is removed.
  - On leave, an empty channel is deleted only if it is in `activeChannels` or in the record.
  - `cleanupOrphans` iterates the guild's records. It deletes empty recorded channels, keeps occupied ones, and removes records whose channel no longer exists. It never scans the category.
  - Deletion helper: a successful delete, or Discord error `10003 Unknown Channel`, removes the record. Any other failure (for example missing permissions) keeps the record so the next startup cleanup retries.
  - New `handleChannelDelete(channelId)` removes the record when a channel is deleted outside the service.
- [apps/bot/src/services.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/apps/bot/src/services.ts) wires the new repository. [apps/bot/src/main.ts](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/apps/bot/src/main.ts) adds a `channelDelete` listener that calls `handleChannelDelete`.

## 3. Roadblocks, Gotchas & Decisions Made
- **Existing databases need `pnpm db:push`** to create `auto_voice_channels`. Until then, creating a channel fails when the record is written (the new channel is then deleted), and leave and cleanup log database errors. A running `next dev` or bot on a shared database needs the push too.
- **One-time consequence of the fix:** temporary channels created before this change have no record. They are no longer deleted automatically and must be removed by hand once. This is the price of never deleting channels the bot cannot prove it created.
- Channel ownership (`activeChannels`: owner, lock state) is still in memory only, as before. After a restart, owner controls do not work in channels created before the restart, but those channels are still deleted when empty. Persisting ownership is out of scope for this bug.
- The record stores no owner on purpose, to keep the table to what the deletion rule needs.
