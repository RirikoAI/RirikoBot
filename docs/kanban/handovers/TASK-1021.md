## 2. Technical Scope & Implementation Completed

### 2.1. 7-Element Affinity Matrix (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/combat/elemental-matrix.ts`)
- Advantage loop granting **1.5x damage multiplier**:
  - `Fire > Ice` (Fire melts Ice)
  - `Ice > Earth` (Ice freezes & fractures Earth)
  - `Earth > Lightning` (Earth grounds & absorbs Lightning)
  - `Lightning > Water` (Lightning electrifies & shocks Water)
  - `Water > Fire` (Water extinguishes Fire)
  - `Light <> Shadow` (Mutual High-Risk Advantage: 1.5x damage to each other)
- Neutral: 1.0x. Disadvantage: 0.75x penalty.
- Includes `getElementalAdvantage`, `getElementalMultiplier`, and descriptive breakdown helpers.

### 2.2. Tactical Status Effects Engine (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/combat/status-effects.ts`)
- **Fire**: *Burn* — Inflicts ongoing damage over time (DoT) equal to 10% of ATK.
- **Ice**: *Freeze & Chill* — Slows opponent's Speed by 25%; 15% chance to Freeze opponent (skipping turn).
- **Earth**: *Fortify* — Provides defensive shielding and reduces physical damage (+20% mitigation).
- **Lightning**: *Surge* — Increases CRIT chance by +15% and causes micro-stuns.
- **Water**: *Purify & Flow* — Restores 8% max HP per turn and mitigates debuff duration.
- **Light**: *Radiance* — Buffs team attack power (+15% ATK) and pierces defense shields.
- **Shadow**: *Decay & Leech* — Absorbs 20% of damage dealt as health regeneration.

### 2.3. Dynamic Battle Perks (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/combat/battle-perks.ts`)
- **Rare**: *Sharpened Edge* (+8% physical DMG against armored targets with DEF >= 100).
- **Super Rare (SR)**: *Vampiric Touch* (Converts 12% of damage dealt into HP healing).
- **Ultra Rare (UR)**: *Glacial Counter* (When attacked, 25% chance to Freeze attacker for 1 turn).
- **Secret Rare (SEC)**: *Mana Conduit* (Reduces skill mana cost by 25% and starts battle with +25 bonus MP).
- **Special Illustration Rare (SIR)**: *Phoenix Ward* (Revives card once per battle with 35% HP upon taking fatal damage).
- **Mythic**: *Cosmic Cataclysm* (Every 3rd turn, automatically unleashes an unblockable true-damage strike dealing 200% ATK ignoring shields and defense).

### 2.4. Combat Simulator Core (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/combat/combat-simulator.ts`)
- Turn queue dynamically sorted by effective Speed (highest acts first).
- Active skills consume MP and deal 1.5x base damage while triggering elemental status effects.
- Basic strikes deal standard physical damage and regenerate 15 MP.
- Turn 10+ Soft Enrage (+100% ATK per turn over turn 9, dealing true damage) to prevent stall loops.
- Support for 1v1 and 3v3 team duels with deterministic RNG injection for 100% test reproducibility.
