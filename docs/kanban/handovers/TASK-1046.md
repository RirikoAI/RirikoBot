## 3. Decisions & Gotchas
- **Collector Persistence**: In Discord.js collectors, calling `collector.stop()` terminates the event listener permanently. In battle flows where post-battle buttons or next-floor actions are needed, the collector must not be stopped when entering the victory/defeat state.
- **Collector Component Types**: Omitting `componentType` from `createMessageComponentCollector` allows listening to both `ComponentType.Button` and `ComponentType.StringSelect` on the same message without running separate collectors.
- **Turn Skips on Consumables**: Consuming an HP or MP potion in battle applies the restorative effect immediately, but relinquishes the player's attack phase, allowing the boss to execute its turn before the next player decision.
