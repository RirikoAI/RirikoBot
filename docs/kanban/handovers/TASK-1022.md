## 2. Technical Scope & Implementation Completed

### 2.1. Player Energy Management (`packages/database/src/repositories/player-energy.repository.ts`)
- Added `consumeEnergy(userId: string, amount: number)`:
  - Validates available energy balance inside ACID transaction.
  - Automatically handles user creation via `getOrCreate`.
  - Rejects actions with clear error messages when energy is insufficient.

### 2.2. Timed Expeditions Engine (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/game-modes/expedition-service.ts`)
- Configured 3 expedition tiers ([docs/waifu-tcg.md:L458–L460](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/docs/waifu-tcg.md#L458-L460)):
  - `1h`: 10 Energy, 150–300 credits, 10–25 dust, 15% card shard chance.
  - `4h`: 25 Energy, 400–800 credits, 50–100 dust, 35% shard chance.
  - `8h`: 45 Energy, 1,000–2,500 credits, 150–300 dust, 70% shard chance.
- Enforces active card exclusivity (cannot deploy the same card concurrently).
- Enforces completion timer before rewards can be claimed.

### 2.3. Cooperative World Boss Raids (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/game-modes/boss-raid-service.ts`)
- World Boss entity with massive HP (e.g. 100,000 HP Leviathan).
- Consumes 30 Energy per attack ([docs/waifu-tcg.md:L465](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/docs/waifu-tcg.md#L465)).
- Runs `CombatSimulator` against the boss and accumulates cumulative damage per player.
- Awards raid badges, credits, and crafting dust scaled to damage dealt.
- Maintains dynamic top raid contributors leaderboard.

### 2.4. PvP Duels & Wager Escrow (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/game-modes/pvp-duel-service.ts`)
- Consumes 5 Energy per ranked duel ([docs/waifu-tcg.md:L466](file:///Z:/Projects/ririko-v2-2026/docs/waifu-tcg.md#L466)).
- Prevents self-duels.
- Validates wallet balances for optional credit wagers.
- Runs `CombatSimulator` with 7-element affinities, tactical status effects, and battle perks.
- Settles wager payouts from loser to winner and updates rating points (+25 rating).

### 2.5. Daily & Weekly Quest Milestones (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/game-modes/quest-service.ts`)
- Canonical quests:
  - `daily_pvp_win`: Win 1 PvP duel (200 credits, 20 dust)
  - `daily_expedition_complete`: Complete 1 Expedition (150 credits, 15 dust)
  - `daily_chat_drop_claim`: Claim 1 Chat Drop (100 credits, 10 dust)
  - `weekly_boss_raid_damage`: Deal 5,000 raid damage (1,000 credits, 100 dust, 1 Summon Ticket)
- Incremental milestone progress tracking and claim verification.

### 2.6. Dual-Dispatch `/game` Command Suite (`apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/game.command.ts`)
- Dual-dispatch parity (Slash and Prefix `!game`):
  - `/game action:pvp user:@Target [wager]`
  - `/game action:explore [duration: 1h|4h|8h] [subaction: start|claim|status]`
  - `/game action:boss [subaction: status|attack]`
  - `/game action:quests [subaction: list|claim] [quest_id]`
