   - `services.eventBus.on('leveling:levelUp', ...)`: `client.guilds.cache.get(guildId)`, then `guild.members.fetch(userId).catch(() => null)`, then `levelRoleService.applyForMember(member, newLevel)`.

**Watch out:** the autorole listener also handles guildMemberAdd. Leave it unchanged; the two are independent.
