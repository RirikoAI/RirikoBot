## 2. Technical Scope & Implementation Completed

### 2.1. Dual-Dialect Repositories (`packages/database/src/repositories/tcg-item.repository.ts`)
- `GameItemRepository`:
  - `findById`, `findByCode`, `findAll` with filtering (`type`, `subtype`, `rarity`, `isShopBuyable`), `create`, `update`, `delete`, `count`.
  - Normalizes `shopPrice` across SQLite (integer) and PostgreSQL (bigint).
- `UserInventoryItemRepository`:
  - `findById`, `findByUser`, `findEquippedByCard`, `findCardSlot`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `count`.
  - `equipToCard`: Atomically unequips any existing item in the target slot and equips the new item.
  - `unequipFromCard`: Unequips item and restores status to `IDLE` with slot `NONE`.

### 2.3. Enhancement Engine (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/equipment/enhancement-service.ts`)
- Upgrades gear from +0 to +10 using Crafting Dust and Credits.
- Cost formula scaled by target level and rarity multiplier (Common 1.0x to Mythic 8.0x).
- Base stats scale linearly by +8% per enhancement level (up to +80% at +10).
- Perk scaling triggers at +5 and +10 breakpoints (`_T2` and `_T3` tags), increasing potency and trigger chances.

### 2.4. 6-Slot Combat Loadouts (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/equipment/loadout-service.ts`)
- Enforces slot-to-subtype constraints across all 6 slots: 3 Equipments (`WEAPON`, `ARMOR`, `RELIC`) and 3 Accessories (`RING`, `AMULET`, `TALISMAN`).
- `getCardLoadout`: Aggregates stats and active battle perks across all equipped items taking into account enhancement levels.
- `applyLoadoutToCombatant`: Directly injects augmented stats (HP, ATK, DEF, SPD, CRIT) and battle perks into the `Combatant` state for battle simulations.

### 2.5. Consumables & Anti-Abuse Engine (`packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/equipment/consumable-service.ts`)
- Consumes HP potions, Mana potions, and Stamina restores.
- Enforces strict anti-abuse limit: maximum **3 Energy Restores per calendar day** using `PlayerEnergyRepository`.
- Cleanses debuffs and status effects with high-tier *Elixir of Full Vitality*.
