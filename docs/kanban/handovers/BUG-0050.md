## GROOMING · 2026-10-21T12:30:00Z · claude-opus-5-5 (coordinator)

**Asked by the maintainer** (2026-10-21): check that the daily energy reward works as intended.

**How it is meant to work** (BUG-0013, `docs/waifu-tcg.md` section 5, `docs/tcg-player-guide.md` section 4)
- At each energy reset day (00:00 GMT+8 by default), the pool refills to `maxEnergy + bonusEnergy`. Energy above that is kept. The potion counter goes back to 0, and `bonusEnergy` grows by `dailyBonusIncrement` (5), up to `maxBonusCap` (50).
- It is lazy: `EnergyLifecycleService.getOrReconcileUserEnergy` (`energy-lifecycle.service.ts:159-213`) applies it on the player's next read or spend. `spendEnergy`, `consumePotion` and `refundEnergy` call it first. That part works: a reproduction on SQLite took a stale 10/100 pool to 105/100 with bonus 5.

**Three paths skip it** (reproduced on in-memory SQLite, 2026-10-21)
1. `apps/bot/src/commands/tcg/dungeon.command.ts:103`: `/dungeon action:status` reads `services.playerEnergyRepo.getOrCreate`. After a reset it shows `10/100` until the player spends energy somewhere else. It also shows `maxEnergy` without the bonus.
2. `packages/services/src/adventure/adventure-engine.ts:174`: `start` charges `this.options.energy.consumeEnergy(input.userId, 15, tx)` straight on the repository. With 10 energy left from yesterday, the start is refused ("you only have 10 Energy") although a reset has passed.
3. `packages/services/src/waifu-tcg/dungeon/dungeon-progress.service.ts:99-104`: the floor 1-10 defeat refund does `currentEnergy: Math.min(energy.maxEnergy, energy.currentEnergy + energyRefunded)`. A player at 105 (100 + bonus 5) drops to 100 after a refund of 5.

**Approach**
1. `EnergyLifecycleService.refundEnergy` (`:250-259`): keep the cap `maxEnergy + bonusEnergy`, and never lower energy: `restored = Math.max(record.currentEnergy, Math.min(cap, record.currentEnergy + amount))`.
2. `DungeonProgressService`: add an optional `energyLifecycle?: EnergyLifecycleService` (a fourth constructor parameter, or turn the optional parameters into an options object if that reads better; keep existing callers working). When it is set, the refund calls `energyLifecycle.refundEnergy(userId, energyRefunded)`. Without it, keep the repository path but cap at `maxEnergy + bonusEnergy` and never lower energy. Pass the lifecycle in `services.ts:979`.
3. `AdventureEngineOptions`: add `reconcileEnergy?: ((userId: string) => Promise<unknown>) | undefined`. In `start`, call it before `this.options.sessions.withUser(...)` (outside the transaction, so a PostgreSQL row lock is never held across two connections). In `services.ts:386`, the engine is built before `energyLifecycleService` (`:477`). Move the lifecycle construction above the engine, or move the engine below it, so `reconcileEnergy: (id) => energyLifecycleService.getOrReconcileUserEnergy(id)` refers to a defined value. Check that nothing between the two depends on the order.
4. `/dungeon action:status`: use `services.energyLifecycleService.getOrReconcileUserEnergy(ctx.user.id)` and show `${currentEnergy}/${maxEnergy + bonusEnergy}`.
5. Tests:
   - `dungeon.command.test.ts`: status with a stale `lastResetDate` shows the refilled pool.
   - `engine.test.ts`: a stale pool of 10 can start once `reconcileEnergy` refills it, and `reconcileEnergy` runs before the charge.
   - `s1-balance-and-progress.test.ts` (the "energy refund (STORY-159)" block): an above-cap pool keeps its energy, and a below-cap pool is refunded up to `maxEnergy + bonusEnergy`.
   - `reset-boundary.test.ts`: `refundEnergy` never lowers energy.

**Pitfalls**
- `AdventurePayoutService` (`adventure-payout.service.ts:161-169`) already caps at `maxEnergy + bonusEnergy` and runs after a start that reconciled. Leave it.
- `PlayerEnergyRepository.consumeEnergyPotion` writes `lastResetDate` with the potion schedule. Leave it. The lifecycle reconciles before every potion in the bot. The coordinator reports the case where the two schedules differ to the maintainer separately.
- Some tests construct `AdventureEngine` and `DungeonProgressService` without the new options. They must keep passing unchanged.

**Out of scope**
- The `/energy` command (TASK-1971). Changes to the energy rules or the bonus design.
