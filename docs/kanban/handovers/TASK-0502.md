## 2. Implemented Features & Changes
1. **CookieRotator Engine (`packages/music/src/extractors/cookie-rotator.ts`)**:
   - Manages a pool of session cookies for YouTube InnerTube/extractor requests.
   - Round-robin selection of valid cookies with usage timestamp tracking.
   - Automatic quarantine logic: after configurable failure threshold (default: 3 failures), cookies are placed in quarantine for a cool-down duration (default: 5 minutes) before being re-evaluated.
   - Dynamic adding, removing, success recovery (`reportSuccess`), and failure tracking (`reportFailure`).
   - Diagnostic metrics: `getStats()` returning `{ total, active, quarantined }`.
2. **Client Spoofing Profiles (`packages/music/src/extractors/client-spoofing.ts`)**:
   - Defined 4 authentic client profiles:
     - `ANDROID`: Pixel 8 Pro / Android 14 client emulation (`19.44.38`).
     - `IOS`: iPhone 15 Pro / iOS 17.5.1 client emulation (`19.45.4`).
     - `TV`: Samsung Smart TV / Tizen 6.0 living-room embedded client (`2.0`).
     - `WEB`: Desktop Chrome 134 Windows 10 client (`2.20260315.01.00`).
   - `buildClientHeaders(clientType, cookie)` generating specialized `User-Agent`, `X-YouTube-Client-Name`, and `X-YouTube-Client-Version` headers.
   - `getFallbackClient(current)`: Automatic fallback cycling across client profiles (`ANDROID` -> `IOS` -> `TV` -> `WEB` -> `ANDROID`).
3. **YouTubeAdapter Integration (`packages/music/src/extractors/youtube.adapter.ts`)**:
   - Initialized with `CookieRotator` and `YouTubeClientType`.
   - `getRequestHeaders()` injecting active rotated cookie and spoofed client headers.
   - `rotateClient()` cycling through client profiles on failure.
4. **ExtractorPipeline Health Summary (`packages/music/src/extractors/pipeline.ts`)**:
   - `getHealthSummary()` providing structured health status across all registered extractors:
     - Status: `HEALTHY`, `DEGRADED`, or `UNHEALTHY`.
     - Health metrics: healthy count, total count, average latency (ms), and timestamp.
5. **Unit Test Suite (`packages/music/src/extractors/cookie-rotator.test.ts`)**:
   - 10 test cases covering round-robin rotation, quarantine backoff, success reset, header building, client fallback cycling, and pipeline health diagnostics.
